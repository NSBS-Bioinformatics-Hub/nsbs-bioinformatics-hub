# 🧬 NSBS National Bioinformatics Hub
**Motto:** *Decoding Life, Empowering the Future*

Welcome to the official digital laboratory and resource center of the **Nigerian Society of Biochemistry Students (NSBS) National Body**. This platform is a strategic initiative designed to bridge the gap between biological research and computational innovation for all biochemistry students across Nigeria.

---

## 🚀 Our Mission
To provide a centralized repository of high-level tools, genomic data, and scientific knowledge, transforming Nigerian biochemistry students into world-class **"Digital Biologists."**

## 🛠️ Technical Toolkit
This hub provides access to:
- **Genomic Programming:** Python scripts for DNA sequence analysis and mutation detection.
- **Data Science:** R and Biostatistics workflows for research validation.
- **Structural Biology:** Resources for Homology Modeling and Molecular Docking.
- **Linux/Bash:** Command-line tools for high-performance biological computing.

## 📚 Learning Resources
- [ ] **NSBS National E-Library:** Access to 50+ world-class Biochemistry & Bioinformatics textbooks.
- [ ] **Research Archive:** Curated international journals on AMR, Sickle Cell, and COVID-19.
- [ ] **Software Links:** Direct access to PyMOL, ChimeraX, and SWISS-MODEL.
