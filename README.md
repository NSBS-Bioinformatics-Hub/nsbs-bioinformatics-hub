# 🧬 NSBS National Bioinformatics Hub
### *“Decoding Life, Empowering the Future”*

Welcome to the **Official Digital Laboratory** of the Nigerian Society of Biochemistry Students (NSBS) National Body. This platform is the bridge between biological data and computational innovation for all biochemistry students in Nigeria.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Vision
Our mission is to transform Nigerian biochemistry students into world-class **Digital Biologists**. We leverage technology to solve healthcare challenges, starting with genomic research and data-driven diagnostics.
